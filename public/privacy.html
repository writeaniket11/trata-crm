Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how TRATA Digital ("we", "us"), reachable at tratadigital.com and hello@tratadigital.com, handles information you share with us through our enquiry forms, including Instagram and Facebook lead forms.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the form: your name, phone number, the type of business you run, the service you need and when you want to start. We also record when the form was submitted and which advertisement it came from.

How we use it

To contact you about your enquiry (by phone or WhatsApp), prepare a quote and follow up. We store it in our internal customer system, which only our team can access with a personal login. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone except service providers that host our systems.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your enquiry and any work we do together, and at most three years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@tratadigital.com or tell us on WhatsApp, and we will do it within 30 days. This is also how to request deletion of data received through Facebook or Instagram.

Security

Our system is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are stored in hashed form and access is limited to our team.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
